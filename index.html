How It Works

From idea to doorstep — a seamless 6-step process.

STEP 01

Concept & Design

Share your idea, sketch, or logo. Our designers create a free digital mockup for your approval.

STEP 02

Quote & Sample

Receive a detailed quote. We produce a physical sample for your approval before mass production.

STEP 03

Tooling & Mold

We craft precision steel dies or silicone molds to ensure every piece is identical to the sample.

STEP 04

Enamel & Finish

Skilled artisans apply enamel colors, electroplating, and protective coatings by hand.

STEP 05

Mass Production

Our factory runs 24/7 production lines to deliver your order on time, every time.

STEP 06

QC & Delivery

Every piece is inspected, packaged with care, and shipped to your door with full tracking.
